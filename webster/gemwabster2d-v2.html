Webster — Speech Synthesizer

Webster features two offline, browser-based synthesis engines.

1. DWM Articulatory Engine (New)

Simulates the physics of sound waves propagating through a 2-D mesh representing the human vocal tract. It uses multiply-free scattering junctions and dynamic impedance mapping. You can manually control the jaw, tongue, and lips in real-time, or type text to watch the physical model articulate the phonemes automatically.

2. Formant Engine (Classic)

Uses a cascade of resonant bandpass filters (formants) excited by oscillators and noise bursts to model speech, typical of 1980s computer voices.
